Gabriele Bianco

I graduated with a Bachelor's in Transportation Design at IAAD (Turin) in July 2023. I now work as a designer at a young cross-disciplinary studio in Turin.

My background combine design, UI / HMI and 3D polygon modelling in Blender — disciplines that, to me, share the same obsession: making complex systems feel intuitive and beautiful.

Outside of work: drawing, reading comics, watching anime, and taking very long walks around the city. I think best on foot.

Experiences

Futura 22 S.R.L. Multidisciplinary Designer Turin, TO // FEB 2024 - TODAY

During my time at Futura22, I had the opportunity to work on a wide range of projects across various fields, including: Automotive Exterior and Interior design, UI/UX design for the automotive industry, configurators and applications, Product and Graphic design and 3D polygonal modeling; involving numerous important client such as Amaro San Simone, Kimera Automobili, Studio FA Porsche, and Italdesign.

Lamborghini (Consultant) Junior Automotive Interior Design Turin, TO // FEB 2026 - MAY 2026

I had the opportunity to join the interior design team of the Lamborghini Centro Stile, based between Turin and Sant’Agata Bolognese, as an external consultant for a project of four months.

Suzuki Italia S.p.A. Automotive Exterior Design Internship Robassomero, TO // OCT 2023 - JAN 2024

Following my graduation, I started an internship at Suzuki Design Europe. I developed completely from scratch a project for a production car exterior, assisted by Suzuki experienced designers. The final output was presented remotely to the Suzuki headquarters in Hamamatsu, Japan.
